A Creve Coeur house or an office: how the same leak plays out

Both need quick action. The difference is who is affected and who must be reached.

Homes and condos

  • One household decides and approves the work
  • Main shutoff is usually near the meter or entry
  • Hot water, kitchen and bath fixtures dominate calls
  • Insurance is typically a homeowners policy

Offices and commercial buildings

  • Landlord, manager and tenants may all need to be reached
  • Shutoffs may be on a riser, in a utility closet or on the roof level
  • Restrooms, break rooms, floor drains and grease lines dominate calls
  • Access, after-hours entry and tenant notices come first
