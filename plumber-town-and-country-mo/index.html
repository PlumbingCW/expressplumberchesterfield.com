Altheim to Town and Country: what a name change means for your pipes

By 1886 this area was the farming community of Altheim. It became Town and Country as a village in 1950 and as a city in 1975. For plumbing, the practical lesson is that a town's age tells you little about a particular house. A lot that was once farmland can hold a house built in any decade, with additions and updates behind the walls.

We do not guess a pipe's age from a neighborhood's history. We trace the lines, camera the sewer when symptoms call for it, and tell you what is actually there. If you plan a remodel, ask us about older lines before you open floors.

Water heater replacement in larger houses

Larger homes can have more bathrooms, longer pipe runs and bigger hot water demand. A tank that served a smaller household may no longer keep up when everyone showers in the morning. When a unit fails, we size the replacement to the fixtures you actually use, not just to the old tank.

Our water heater replacement work includes removing the old unit, setting the new one, and connecting gas, venting or electric to code. If your system is newer, repair may be the better answer. We say which one when we see it.

Repair the sewer line or replace it

Sewage in the yard, roots in a camera picture or repeated clogs may all lead to this decision.

Spot repair or lining

  • Fits a single break, a bad joint or a short root-damaged stretch
  • Less digging and shorter disruption
  • The camera must show the rest of the line is sound
  • See sewer line repair for the options

Full replacement

  • Fits a line that is collapsed or broken in many places
  • More digging and restoration
  • A longer-lasting result when the whole run is failing
  • Often follows a sewer inspection camera pass
