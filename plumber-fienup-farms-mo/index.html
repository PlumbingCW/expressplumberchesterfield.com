Fienup Farms is a 223-acre planned residential subdivision north of Wild Horse Creek Road, between Long Road and Baxter Road. The City of Chesterfield approved its first six plats on March 4, 2019, covering 111 of the 223 lots, so its homes are young. How the subdivision is laid out, from city records

Worth knowing

City plat documents place Fienup Farms north of Wild Horse Creek Road, with Long Road to the west and Baxter Road to the east. Streets named in those records include Fienup Farms Boulevard at the west entrance and Fienup Lakes Drive, and a lake is part of the third plat. A seventh plat of 15 lots was reviewed in May 2019.

For a service call, that means one thing. Give your street and lot number, not only the subdivision name, because dispatch confirms coverage by address.

Plumbing in a home that is only a few years old

Read the builder warranty before you hire anyone. Warranties often name who may do certain repairs, and a repair by someone else can complicate a claim. A plumber can still diagnose an active problem and tell you what to bring to the builder.

Missouri American Water's winter guidance applies to young homes as much as old ones. Know where the main shutoff is and test it. Insulate exposed pipes in garages and along exterior walls, and drain and turn off any irrigation system before a hard freeze.
