How a drain cleaning visit goes in Ballwin

1

Find where it stops

We run water at the fixtures and listen. A single sink points to a branch, while several fixtures point to the main.

2

Choose the right tool

A hand auger clears a sink trap line. A drain machine handles the main. Roots or grease heavy buildup may call for a hydro jetter.

3

Clear and test

We run the line after clearing to see that water moves normally and does not back up.

4

Camera if it keeps coming back

A line that clogs twice in a year probably has a defect. A camera shows a belly, a break or roots.

5

Tell you what to do next

If the line needs repair, we explain the options, including a sewer line repair only when the camera proves it.
