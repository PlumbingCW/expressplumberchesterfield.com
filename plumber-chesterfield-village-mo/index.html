Chesterfield Village is the planned community developer Louis Sachs began assembling in the 1960s, with Chesterfield Mall, which opened in 1976, as one of its early anchors. If you live or work there and need a plumber in Chesterfield Village, MO, your problems are shaped by shared buildings more than by single-family yards.

We found no official boundary map for the Village, so this page uses the anchors that published sources name: Highway 40 at Clarkson and Olive roads, and Justus Post Road. Give your street address when you call and dispatch will confirm coverage.

What the published record says about the Village

St. Louis Public Radio reports that Sachs bought 37 acres at Highway 40 and Clarkson and Olive roads in 1967. His first Village project was a 30,000 square foot office building at 16300 Justus Post Road in 1975, and the mall opened a year later.

At his death in 2011 the Village was described as more than 200 businesses and 260 homes. A second source describes condominiums, apartments and office buildings around a regional mall. Those counts are a 2011 snapshot, but both descriptions point to a mixed community, and that matters because a leak in one condo can reach the unit below.

Condo owner or office tenant: who usually handles what

Governing documents and leases set the real line. This is the usual split, so read yours before you pay for a repair.

Usually the owner or tenant

  • Faucets, toilets and fixtures inside the unit
  • A water heater that serves only your unit, if your documents say so
  • The drain from your fixtures up to the shared line
  • Damage to your own belongings and finishes

Usually the association or property manager

  • Main water risers and shared drain stacks
  • Building shutoff valves and common-area pipes
  • Mechanical rooms and shared water heating
  • Repairs that affect more than one unit
