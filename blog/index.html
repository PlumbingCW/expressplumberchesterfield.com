This is the plumbing blog for Chesterfield Express Plumbing, written for homeowners in Chesterfield, MO and nearby towns. The posts below explain common problems in plain language, say what you can safely check yourself and say clearly when to stop and call. Think of them as a first read before you pick up the phone.

Every post is general guidance. Your pipes, fixtures and house are unique, so a plumber who sees the problem in person will always know more than an article.

What the posts cover

Most topics follow what people actually run into. Drains that slow down or smell, hot water that runs out too soon, a ceiling stain with no obvious source, and pipes that freeze when the temperature drops. Each post names the tools a plumber would use, such as a drain machine, a pipe camera or a pressure gauge.

Some posts draw on local facts, for example Missouri American Water's 2021 quality report for the St. Louis region or the City of Chesterfield's sewer lateral repair program. Those details can change, so we point you to the source to check the current version.

Where to start by problem

Slow or smelly drains

Begin with how drain cleaning works and when a camera look through sewer inspection is the better first step.

Hot water trouble

Read about tank failure signs before you call for water heater repair, and compare that with water heater replacement when the unit is old.

Hidden water

A rising bill or damp wall calls for leak detection, and a split line in winter calls for burst pipe repair.

Wet basements

A basement that floods after storms is a case for sump pump installation and repair, often paired with grading or waterproofing questions.

How to use these guides well

  • ✓
    Read for the decision, not the diagnosisA post can tell you whether a problem is urgent. It cannot tell you exactly what is wrong inside your wall.
  • ✓
    Shut off first, read secondIf water is moving, close the main valve before reading anything. The posts will still be here.
  • ✓
    Check local sourcesPermit rules, water quality and city programs change. Ask the City of Chesterfield, St. Louis County or your water utility for the latest.
  • ✓
    Call when you reach the stop lineEach post says where DIY ends. Past that point, call the 24/7 line and describe what you found.

Guidance versus a plumber's visit

A blog post cannot find a leak, see inside a drain or test a gas connection. It can help you decide how urgent something is, what to have ready for the call and what a fair repair looks like. We list cost factors, never job prices, because the right number depends on your home.

If you want to talk through a problem, call and describe it. You hear a clear price before work starts. The services page lists everything we handle, and the Wildwood and Town and Country pages show nearby towns we serve.
